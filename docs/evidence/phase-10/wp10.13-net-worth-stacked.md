# WP 10.13: Net worth stacked by account type (V6 of the visual programme)

**Request (Chris, verbatim):** "Dispatch v1 and once complete continue thru v8", after "i really like the visuals within monarch money when it comes to spend tracking and other financial health … identify strategic visual improvements to more formally mirror it." V6 is item six in §3 of `docs/research/10-visual-design-notes.md`. §1.4 "Net worth" describes Monarch's version: `NetWorthChart` → its stacked bar chart, bars stacked by account type with a net line over them, `borderRadius` and `opacity`, the scrubbable its net worth widget with period chips and a delta in money and percent, and a milestone screen.

**Result.** The dashboard's **Net worth** card and the **full history** dialog now show net worth as **stacked bars by account type**. Each bar has one segment per account type, coloured by `accountTypeHue`: property, cash, crypto, investments and other above the zero line, and liabilities (negative) below it. Only the topmost positive and bottommost negative segments get rounded 4 px ends, and a 2 px surface gap separates segments. A **net line** with ringed dots, drawn in `--chart-line`, sits over the bars. The period chips are V2's (**1M 3M 6M 1Y All**), and each period has its own bucketing: by day, by ISO week or by month. The table caption states the bucketing rule.

**Scrub:** hovering, or pressing ← → Home End on the focused chart (a `slider` whose value text is announced, plus a polite live region), selects a bucket:

- buckets older than the selection are dimmed and a crosshair marks the selected one;
- the readout shows the date, the net, and the change since the previous bucket and since the first bucket, in money and percent. The percent is withheld, with a stated reason, when the base is zero or negative;
- the legend lists each account type present with its total at that bucket;
- the hero figure (`NetWorthHero`) follows the selection.

Leaving the plot, pressing Escape or moving focus away returns everything to the latest bucket, after the 250 ms grace period that 1.4.13 requires. "Live, may still change" stays on the hero.

**Milestones:** the snapshotter records each crossing of $10k / $25k / $50k / $100k / every further $100k, and of zero in either direction. Each crossing is recorded **once per holder** (`0049`, a unique index) and emits `planning.NetWorthMilestoneCrossed`. The next time the dashboard loads, it shows a one-line notice ("You crossed $200,000 in net worth on Oct 15") with a **Dismiss** that the server remembers. There is no confetti.

**Data:** the existing `GET /net-worth/series` takes an optional `bucket=day|week|month` and returns `buckets[]` with segments by account type. They are computed in **one read** of `net_worth_daily` in `@ctx/planning`. On the wire, every bucket's segments sum exactly to the series' own value on the bucket's closing day.

**Not done by this WP, by design.**

- **No hatched "not converted" segment.** The dispatch asked for one only "if the amount is known in its own currency and a rate is simply missing". The read model can never hold that case. `computeNetWorth` throws `FxRateUnavailable` and **the day is not written at all** (D4; `net-worth.ts`, `snapshot.ts` "Failure posture"), and every stored breakdown entry already carries `baseMinor` with its `fxRate`. A bucket is therefore always drawn from the days that are known. Two notes say what is left out:
  - the existing `lastSnapshotFailure` note, unchanged and still under the widget's chart;
  - a new one for a window that spans a base-currency change. Each currency gets its own chart, and a currency that no bucket at the chosen scale closes on is **named** ("Not drawn at this scale: CAD — … choose 1M to see every day").

  A hatch with nothing that could ever fill it would be dead code, and the dataviz skill lists texture-by-default as an anti-pattern.

- **The event is `planning.NetWorthMilestoneCrossed`, not `networth.MilestoneCrossed`.** The net-worth context is `@ctx/planning`, and `phase3-registration.test.ts` asserts that every Planning event type starts with `planning.`.
- **The Brief has no notable-events section.** Its sections are the attention strip, the ranked advisor findings and the health link card (`BriefScreen.tsx`), so milestones are not added to it.
- **The "toast" is an inline one-line notice at the top of the dashboard**, not a `sonner` toast. A sonner toast times out on its own, so a holder could miss it before choosing to dismiss it. The notice is `role="status"` and stays until dismissed. After a dismissal, focus moves to `#main-content`.
- **Downward round figures are not milestones.** Only zero counts going down (the dispatch: "… then every $100k; and the zero line in either direction"). A dip back under $50k says nothing.
- **The selected bucket is marked by a 1 px crosshair, not a band.** A `--bg-secondary` band was tried first and `contrast.test.ts` refused it: the orange fill falls to **2.93:1** on it in the light theme.
- **The stacking order is not the dispatch's listing order.** From the zero line up it is property, cash, crypto, investments, other, chosen so that every adjacent pair clears the validator (§1.4). Brown next to neutral (ΔE 7.8 normal-vision) or next to dark orange (10.1) would not.
- **The full history gained its own hero.** It had none, so "the hero follows the selection" had nothing to follow there. It shows the same `NetWorthHero`, driven by its own scrub.
- **The full history's chips are V2's.** URL values are unchanged: `netWorthRange=p30d|p90d|p1y|all` still work, and `p182d` was added for 6M.
- **The readout is always shown.** With nothing selected it describes the latest bucket and says "latest". While a bucket is selected it is the chart's 1.4.13 readout: hoverable, persistent, and dismissed by Escape.
- **`net_worth_milestones` is not exported** (exempt in `registry.ts`: it is derived from `net_worth_daily`), so a restore does not carry dismissals.
- **No `docs/design/tokens.md` change: no role was added.** `net-worth-chart-tokens.ts` maps existing roles (`--chart-line`, `--bg-primary`, `--chart-negative-area`, `--chart-axis`, `--chart-gridline`, `--chart-label`, `--text-primary`). The segments use the V1 fills through `accountTypeHue`.
- **The sweep's default net-worth fixture is now the stacked holder.** `installSweepMocks(page, { networth: {} })` restores WP 3.12's five days. As a result `/` (and `/plan`'s sealed start) in the a11y sweep draw real bars and the milestone notice.

### 1.1 The bucketing rule (stated in the table caption)

| Period  | Bucket                     | Caption                                                                                                                                        |
| ------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1M      | calendar day               | "One bar per day."                                                                                                                             |
| 3M, 6M  | ISO week, Monday to Sunday | "One bar per week (Monday to Sunday), showing the last recorded day of the week; the first and last weeks are cut at the edges of the period." |
| 1Y, All | calendar month             | "One bar per month, showing the last recorded day of the month; the first and last months are cut at the edges of the period."                 |

A bucket's figure is the **closing** one: the current revision of the last day in the bucket, inside the window, that has a snapshot. Net worth is a stock, so a bucket's figure is never a sum or an average. A bucket with no snapshot is a gap (nulls, no segments) and is never shown as zero. For All, leading gaps before the first snapshot are dropped. Days are the holder's own calendar days (`as_of_date` is a local business date). In SQL the week is `as_of_date - (isodow - 1)` and the month is `date_trunc('month', as_of_date::timestamp)::date`. In TS it is `bucketStartOf`, and the weekly-boundary tests run both. The mock's copy (`networth-buckets.ts`) follows the same rule.

**Segments.** A segment is the sum of the closing day's breakdown entries with `inNetWorth` set, grouped by type. The **side decides first**: every liability-side entry is `liabilities`, because `liabilities_minor` is summed by side. Next comes the ledger's type and subtype: depository → cash; investment → investments; crypto → crypto; manual with `property|real_estate|home|vehicle` → property; any other → other. For an account the ledger no longer lists, Planning's valuation class decides (cash → cash, investment → investments, else other). Σ segments = `net_worth_minor` holds by construction (both are sums over the same `inNetWorth` entries, liabilities signed), and is tested.

### 1.2 The milestone ladder

- **Up:** $10k, $25k, $50k, $100k, then every $100k, in major units of the base currency. An up-crossing of `m` is `previous < m <= latest`.
- **Zero:** in either direction. Down is `previous >= 0 > latest`.
- **Several at once:** a jump crosses every rung between: 20k → 60k gives 25k and 50k, and −5k → 12k gives zero-up and 10k.
- **What is compared:** the open day (the latest) against the current revision of the most recent earlier day. A different base currency, or no earlier day, means no crossing.
- **Once:** each milestone is recorded once per holder, ever. The unique index `(user_id, milestone_minor, direction)` enforces it, and the event is appended only for rows the insert returned. The two CHECKs (`down_is_zero_only`, `figures_cross`) restate the ladder in the database.

### 1.3 Colour (the dataviz validator, both themes)

The fills are V1's fixed account hues. Only the stacking order was chosen here. `validate_palette.js` (the skill's) was run over the order as stacked, against `--bg-primary`:

| dispatch order: cash, investments, crypto, property, other | **FAIL**: brown↔neutral ΔE 7.8 normal, 5.6 protan                           | **FAIL**: brown↔neutral 8.7 normal, 6.0 protan         |

Light's 7.3 is in the validator's 6–8 band, which is "legal ONLY with secondary encoding". The validator's "chroma floor" fails for neutral ("other") and brown in both orders. Those hues are V1's fixed mapping (neutral is "other" by design) and are not changed by this WP.

- The bucketed read adds about **6 ms at p50** (74–76 vs 68–70).
- The occasional 160–290 ms stalls are endpoint-wide. Run 5 caught one in the no-bucket read, and instrumenting the route showed the four reads finishing in at most 69 ms even on the slow requests. The stall is therefore after the reads: serialising and validating 3,652 daily points. A stall is one or two samples in thirty; when two land in the same thirty, p95 (the 29th sample) goes over.
- So the budget **holds at p50 with wide margin and is flaky at p95** on this host, and the cause predates this WP.

The reads finish within 69 ms, so the stall is serialising and validating 3,652 daily points. The fix is a contract change: trim `points` for long windows, or return All's daily points only on request.

After a base-currency change, a figure already crossed in the old currency is never announced in the new one. Detection also refuses to compare two days in different currencies.
