# WP 10.14: Category detail and emoji icons (V7 of the visual programme)

**Request (Chris, verbatim):** "Dispatch v1 and once complete continue thru v8", following "i really like the visuals within monarch money when it comes to spend tracking and other financial health … identify strategic visual improvements to more formally mirror it." V7 is the seventh item in §3 of `docs/research/10-visual-design-notes.md`. Its sources are Monarch's equivalent / its history chart (§1.4) and the emoji `Category.icon` (§1.4, "Transactions").

**Result.** `/categories/:id` is a category's own screen. It opens from every category name: the Cash Flow breakdown rows (by category and by group), the Budget rows, the Reports tables (the category-by-month table and budget-vs-actual), and the Transactions filter when exactly one category is chosen.

- **Header.** The category's icon in a circle of its hue, its name, its group and kind, its variability chip (V4) and an **Edit** button.
- **Hero.** This month so far, then the sentence "This month vs. average month (last 12 months)" stated in money with a direction word ("So far this month, $146.67 less than your average month of $356.67."). The sentence is withheld with the reason when the ledger has fewer than 2 complete months.
- **Chart.** Monthly bars for the 12 complete months plus this one, in the category's hue. This month is hatched and marked "so far". The 12-complete-month average is a dashed line in `--chart-label`, labelled with its amount. A month with no spend is drawn as a 2 px zero stub on the baseline and listed as "No spend".
- **Selecting a month.** A press on a bar, or ← → Home End on the focused chart (a slider whose value text is the month's sentence, announced), selects a month. The selection is URL state (`?month=`). It updates the readout and filters the transactions list beneath to the category and that month. That list is `/transactions`' own `TransactionsList`, bulk bar and detail sheet, not a fork. "See transactions" is the old drill-through, for the selected month.
- **Hover and table.** Hovering shows a 1.4.13 readout. "View as table" lists every month.
- **Emoji icons.** Each category can store one emoji (`categories.emoji`, migration 0050). It is validated as exactly one emoji by one shared rule, `isSingleEmoji` in `@core/kernel`, on the server and in the editor. It goes through POST, PATCH, export and import.
- **Default icons.** A default table covers all 153 shipped category keys. It is a display fallback only and is never written. A custom category with no emoji shows its initials.
- **Where `CategoryIcon` appears.** The emoji in a circle tinted by the category's hue appears in the category picker, the transactions rows (each row now also names its category), the Cash Flow breakdowns, the Budget rows, the Reports tables, the Inbox review rows and the detail header.
- **Editor.** `CreateCategoryDialog` is now a create/edit dialog. It has the name, the group and kind (read-only when editing, because PATCH cannot move a category), and an icon radio grid with a free-text field and "Use default". It also has a radio group of 13 hue swatches, each named, with the current one checked and ticked, and the V4 classify control used as a form value. It saves through PATCH with only the fields that changed.

**State of the tree.** Uncommitted, on `main`. Dispatched at `a225e3f`; HEAD is now `24ebd86`, the orchestrator's execution-log commit, which touches none of these files. Nothing is staged by this WP. Migration `0050_category_icon.sql` is expand-only (DDL in §5). No new runtime or dev dependency and no lockfile change. The backend half was built by a sub-agent against the running dev database with scratch databases only; I re-ran its suites myself (§2).

**Not done, by design.**

- **The column is `emoji`, not `icon`.** `categories.icon` already exists: it is NOT NULL with default `circle`, holds a Lucide icon name (D20), and is on the wire as `icon`. Repurposing it would be a contract change, not an expand. The migration file keeps the brief's name, `0050_category_icon.sql`.
- **8 code points is a hard ceiling (the brief's CHECK).** It refuses a few real single emoji. For example, the kiss with two skin tones, 🧑🏻‍❤️‍💋‍🧑🏼, is 10 code points. Tag-sequence flags (7) and four-person families (7) fit. `isSingleEmoji` enforces the same bound, so a too-long emoji is a 400, never a 23514.
- **Text-presentation pictographs such as © and ® are accepted.** They are `Extended_Pictographic`, so the rule takes them without VS16.
- **History starts at the ledger's first row, not at the category's first row.** A month counts as "in history" from the month of the earliest ledger row (any category, any currency). Months before that have no bar, are excluded from the average and are listed as "Before your ledger's first transaction". The alternative, starting at the category's first row, would drop real zero months, which the brief rules out ("zero months count"). A custom category created last week is therefore averaged over the ledger's months, its zeros included.
- **The ledger's first month counts as a whole month,** even if the first row is mid-month.
- **A refund is netted only within its purchase's month.** This matches the Cash Flow hub's per-month breakdown, and the route test checks it against `GET /reports/cash-flow` month by month.
- **Archived categories have no screen.** `GET /categories` omits them by default. A transaction row in an archived category shows "Archived category" with its id's tint.
- **No emoji-picker library.** The grid is the default table's distinct emoji, at most 48, plus a free-text field.
- **The hero amount uses `Money`'s `tabular-nums`,** as the brief asks. V5 chose proportional figures for its 56 px score; this hero is a money figure and follows the house `Money` component.
