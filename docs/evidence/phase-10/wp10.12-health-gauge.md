### 1.1 The tier words and the next step

| Tier         | Score          | Quartet | Sentence                      |
| ------------ | -------------- | ------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Building** | under 50       | warning | "You're building the basics." |
| **Steady**   | 50 to under 80 | success | "You're on steady ground."    |
| **Strong**   | 80 and over    | info    | "You're in strong shape."     |

Then "The one thing to do next: <step> (<pillar>)". The pillar is the lowest component score; ties go to the higher weight, then catalogue order. The step's target is the **next-better band edge from the wire's thresholds**, e.g. "save 10% of your income", "build your cash toward 6 months of spending", "bring minimum payments under 36% of income". All pillars excellent, and no score at all, each have their own honest sentence. The boundaries 50/80 are presentation, not scoring: the band scores are 25/50/75/100, so all-fair is Steady, all-good (75) is Steady, and Strong needs some excellents.

### 1.2 The living-expenses rule

- **Fixed:** Σ net outflow in categories classified `fixed`, over the **three complete calendar months** before today's month, ÷ 3.
- **The fixed share of non-monthly:** Σ net outflow in `non_monthly` categories over the **twelve complete months** before today's month, ÷ 12 — an annual or irregular bill spread over the year is what a month must carry for it; a three-month window would miss it or triple it.
- **Living expenses per month = fixed + that share.** Flexible and unclassified categories never count. Today's (partial) month never counts.
- Net outflow per category = −Σ Reports' signed cells (transfers out, refunds netted), floored at zero per category. Integer half-even division in minor units.
- **Withheld** when no category is classified `fixed` or `non_monthly` (unlock: "Classify your categories on the Budget screen.", linked to `/budget?view=variability`), or when those categories had no spending in their windows.
- **Living cover** = cash ÷ living expenses, one decimal, half-even, banded on the emergency-fund ladder, shown as "shown, not scored".

### 1.3 Gauge, zones and sparklines against the dataviz skill

Form: the score is a single value against a 0–100 scale with three named states — a meter, drawn as Monarch's arc. The track segments are each tier's `-bg` wash with surface notches at 50 and 80, the fill the current tier's `-bold` (status colours are reserved for state, always with the word). The hero number is ≥ 48 px, proportional figures. Zones: horizontal bands in their band's `-bg`, band words in the same quartet's `-text`, the metric as a 2 px `--chart-line` with the "Now" point ringed in `--bg-primary`; one axis; hover readout and a table view. ## 5. Migration DDL

```sql
CREATE TABLE health_score_snapshots (
  id           UUID         PRIMARY KEY,
  user_id      UUID         NOT NULL REFERENCES users (id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  as_of_date   DATE         NOT NULL,
  score        NUMERIC(5,2) CHECK (score >= 0 AND score <= 100),
  pillars      JSONB        NOT NULL
                            CONSTRAINT health_score_snapshots_six_pillars
                            CHECK (jsonb_typeof(pillars) = 'array' AND jsonb_array_length(pillars) = 6),
  computed_at  TIMESTAMPTZ  NOT NULL,
  created_at   TIMESTAMPTZ  NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),
  CONSTRAINT health_score_snapshots_score_requires_pillar CHECK (
    (score IS NULL) = (NOT jsonb_path_exists(pillars, '$[*] ? (@.status == "scored")'))
  )
);

CREATE UNIQUE INDEX health_score_snapshots_user_date_idx
  ON health_score_snapshots (user_id, as_of_date);
```

Plus three `COMMENT ON` statements (table, `score`, `pillars`). Expand-only: one new table, nothing altered or dropped, nothing backfilled; references only `users`.
