# WP 10.7: The Recurring screen's Forward cash flow, redrawn

**Request (Chris, verbatim, with a screenshot of the Recurring screen in dark theme):** "the re-occurring tab chart has some numbers overlapping. create a stronger graphical view that corrects this."

**Result.** The three figures no longer overlap, and no stat tile anywhere can collapse onto its neighbours: `StatTile` now has an intrinsic width of its own (`contain-intrinsic-inline-size`, a `min-width` capped at the parent's width, `flex: 1 1 auto`), so it keeps its width in a row that sizes children by their content. The Forward cash flow card is rebuilt by the dataviz method: a stat strip (a grid sized by the card: one column, then the hero across two, then three) with Safe to spend as the only hero figure and Expected net and Worst case subordinate, each with a line saying what it means; and a chart in which the running total is a **step line** that drops on the day a charge is due, the detector's uncertainty band around it, a labelled $0 baseline, a light money y-axis, x ticks whose first and last labels are anchored inward, and **a mark per expected charge on its due date** (▼ money out, ▲ money in, coloured by sign), with each day's charges named in the readout and in the data table. A window with no charge draws no line and says why, naming the streams outside it; a window whose every charge is fixed says the band is empty because nothing is uncertain. Reviewing the chart's input turned up a real defect (§0 (c)): the chart negated an outflow twice, so a real outflow would have plotted as money in. Fixed and pinned.

**State of the tree.** Uncommitted, on `main` at `e46d56f`. Nothing staged by this WP. Web only (`apps/web`, `docs/`): no migration, no API or contract change, no dependency.

**Not done by design.** No real stack or database was used: every check runs against the web package's own mocks (`apps/web/e2e/support/mock-recurring-insights.ts` and the sweep's layered mocks). The live check is Chris's next look at Recurring on his own data. The original screenshot is not in the repo; §4's "before" is the description below. `~/personalfi-prod`, project `personalfi-prod`, its volumes, images and secrets were not touched. No `docker` command was run.

## 0. Before, and the diagnosis

**Before** (the request's screenshot, dark theme, sandbox data: one yearly $5.86 charge with no due date in the 30-day window, so the projection was all zeros): the three tiles of the Forward cash flow card were drawn on top of one another. The labels ran together as "SafeExpeWorst / to net (30e / spendays)" and the figures "+$67,498.00" and "$0.00" overlapped. Below them the band chart was a flat blue line at $0 with a large empty area; the x ticks ran Oct 18 … "Nov 1!", the last tick cut off at the right edge; "View as table" under it.

| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## 1. What was built

### The method (dataviz skill, in its order)

1. **Form.** The headline numbers are a KPI row of stat tiles, not a chart: one hero (Safe to spend), two subordinate. The 30-day cash flow's job is _trend over time, against a baseline, with discrete events_: a line against $0, single series (no legend box needed for the line alone, but the marks and band make four things, so a key is drawn), with the events as marks. The running total is a step function (money leaves on a day), so the line is `curveStepAfter`, not the old monotone curve that drew money leaving gradually.
2. **Colour by job.** One series (the running total) in the accent, slot 1; its band a ~12 % wash of the same hue; a charge's _sign_ in the house CVD-safe money pair (`--money-neg` vermilion for out, `--money-pos` teal for in), never red/green; chrome in `--chart-gridline` / `--chart-axis-ink`. Text never wears a series colour: tick labels and legend text are text tokens, the colour sits in the key beside them.
3. **Validated, not eyeballed** (the skill's `scripts/validate_palette.js`, `--pairs all` because the three can sit anywhere next to each other):

   | Mode  | Palette (line, out, in) on surface     | Band | Chroma | CVD worst pair          | Normal-vision worst pair | Contrast        |
   | ----- | -------------------------------------- | ---- | ------ | ----------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------- |
   | light | `#2563eb,#d2452f,#0f8a68` on `#ffffff` | PASS | PASS   | ΔE 9.9 (deutan, out↔in) | ΔE 25.2                  | PASS, all ≥ 3:1 |
   | dark  | `#3987e5,#e8604a,#1ba277` on `#16181a` | PASS | PASS   | ΔE 8.8 (deutan, out↔in) | ΔE 20.5                  | PASS, all ≥ 3:1 |

   Both exit 0 with every check PASS (CVD ≥ 8 target, not the 6–8 warn band). Sign also has a secondary encoding: ▼ out, ▲ in.

4. **Marks.** 2 px line, round joins; band at 12 % opacity; solid 1 px hairline gridlines (never dashed; the old dashed zero line and dashed crosshair are gone); the $0 baseline a step darker and its label semibold; charge marks 10 × 9 px triangles with a 2 px surface ring (`paint-order: stroke`) so stacked marks on one day stay apart; a hairline stem from each charge day's lane up to the line, so each step has its cause under it.
5. **Hover.** One pointer surface over the plot and the lane; the crosshair snaps to the nearest day; the readout (pinned to the top of the plot, flipped left past the middle by `TooltipWithBounds`, so it never covers the lane or runs off the chart) leads with the value: the day, the running total, its band, then each charge that day as "▼ −$65.00 Fernwave Mobile". WCAG 1.4.13 rules (`useChartTooltipRules`) are unchanged.
6. **Accessibility.** `role="img"` with a name that now carries the window, the number of charges, the net and the worst case ("Projected cash flow from Jun 1 to Jul 1: 6 expected charges, expected net −$1,626.99, worst case −$1,664.99."). The data table (09 §5.6) gains a **Charges due** column per day ("Fernwave Mobile −$65.00; Metro Water −$42.00 (−$50.00 to −$36.00)", "—" on a day with none). Figures are tabular everywhere, as the request asks (the skill would keep proportional figures on a lone hero number; the request and WP 10.2's decision win). Nothing animates (no transition, no animation; the sweep's reduced-motion check is green).
7. **Looked at.** Every screenshot in §4 was opened and checked for collisions and overflow before this file was written; the first render showed the line starting already $1,450 down (the rent on day one had no visible drop), fixed by drawing from $0 at the first day.

## 5. For Chris on first use

- With the sandbox as in the screenshot, the card will show the three figures side by side and, in place of the chart, "No charges expected in the next 30 days — 1 stream (Uber, yearly) has no due date in this window."
- When a month has charges, each one is a small ▼ (out) or ▲ (in) under the chart on its due date; hover a day or open **View as table** to see which.
- If the chart ever shows money **in** where you expect a bill, that is the defect in §0 (c) and it is fixed here; say so if you see it.
