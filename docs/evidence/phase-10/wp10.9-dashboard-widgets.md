# WP 10.9: The dashboard as widgets, with the spending pace (V2 of the visual programme)

**Request (Chris, verbatim):** "Dispatch v1 and once complete continue thru v8" — after "i really like the visuals within monarch money when it comes to spend tracking and other financial health … identify strategic visual improvements to more formally mirror it." V2 is §3's second item of `docs/research/10-visual-design-notes.md`: the dashboard as a customisable widget grid, centred on Monarch's equivalent (this month's cumulative spend by day against last month's, from a `byDay` aggregate with `fillEmptyValues`).

**Result.** `/` is a grid of eight self-contained widgets in a common `DashboardWidgetCard` frame (title, subtitle, "See all" to the owning screen, and honest loading / empty / error states): **Net worth** (the existing chart and full-history dialog, now with 1M 3M 6M 1Y All chips and the change over the period in money and percent), **Spending pace** (the centrepiece), **Budgets** (Left to budget and the three envelopes closest to or over budget as meters), **Upcoming** (the next 7 days of expected charges, Safe to spend, and an overdraft warning in words), **To review** (the inbox's own queue count), **Goals** (progress per active goal), **Investments** (total value, the change since yesterday or why there is none, "no price yet" instead of $0) and **Top insights**. The grid is one column on a phone, two at tablet width, three on a 1280 px desktop (sized by its container, so the sidebar and zoom behave like a narrower screen); net worth spans two. **Customize** (a dialog: a Show switch and Move up / Move down per widget, Reset to default, all keyboard-operable, no drag) saves order and visibility per holder to `users.dashboard_layout` (migration `0046`). A hidden widget mounts nothing and fetches nothing. The pace is served by a new `GET /reports/spending-pace` that runs **Reports' own spending predicate day by day**; its last point _is_ the month's `GET /reports/spending` total, asserted. Building it found three real things (§0).

**State of the tree.** Uncommitted, on `main` (dispatched at `5342a1f`; HEAD is now `9c2d390`, the orchestrator's execution-log commit, which touches none of these files). Nothing staged by this WP. One expand-only migration (`0046_dashboard_layout.sql`, DDL in §5). No new runtime or dev dependency; no lockfile change. The 16 PNGs under `docs/evidence/phase-10` that are not this WP's (`wp10.3-*` modified, `wp3.11-*` untracked) were not touched; this WP's four screenshots were written by a targeted run only (`-g "WP 10.9 evidence"`).

**Not done by design.**

- **No category-level "excluded from reports" flag exists** (checked: `categories` has `kind`, `hue`, `is_system`, `archived_at`). The pace honours what Reports honours: transfer legs, the user's per-transaction exclusion, off-budget accounts, income categories, a net-positive uncategorised bucket. Adding such a flag is a schema decision for a later package.
- **Pending rows: the one deliberate difference from Reports.** Reports counts pending rows; the pace leaves them out by default (as dispatched) and says how many ("1 pending charge is not counted until it posts"). `?includePending=true` makes the two identical, and the parity test uses it.
- **The layout is not exported.** It lives on `users`, which `registry.ts` exempts from the bundle (like `display_timezone`); there is no other per-holder preferences row (`narrator_settings` is the narrator's own). Recorded below as an ORCHESTRATOR line.
- **Goal progress is computed in the web**, by `@ctx/planning`'s own funded rule for a goal without earmarks (`evaluateGoalPace`: positive balances of the asset accounts it names, an account two active goals share counted for neither, never across currencies), because `GET /goals` carries no funded figure and earmarks have no route. It is a mirror, said in the code and here; a `funded` field on `GET /goals` would retire it (ORCHESTRATOR line).
- **"Left to budget" is the budget's own Ready to Assign** (`period.readyToAssign`), not a new computation: in this envelope budget, Ready to Assign is income not yet given a job after every assignment, savings envelopes included — what Monarch's "income − budgeted expenses − planned savings" means here.
- **The projected lowest balance** (Upcoming's overdraft line) is computed in the web from `GET /recurring/safe-to-spend`'s `availableCash` and `GET /recurring/upcoming`'s per-day expected outflows and inflows over the same 30 days the Recurring screen reads (Monarch's equivalent). The server has no such field.
- **No new tokens or roles.** The widget card is the existing `Card`; `docs/design/tokens.md` needs no widget-card note.
- README has no dashboard paragraph to update (its only mention is the install walk-through landing "at the dashboard").

## 0. What building it found

- **(a) The pace's first read was over budget on a busy ledger.** On the 50k seed as generated (~40 rows a month: 1,264 months) the route was ~27 ms p95; on the same 50,040 rows mapped onto 24 months (~2,000 a month, 6,300 rows in the three-month read) it was **229 ms p95** against the 150 ms budget. Dropping keyset paging for one statement per series alone gave 217 ms; an in-process profile showed the full-row read and `LedgerTransaction` mapping were ~75 of ~110 ms. Fix: `@ctx/ledger`'s **`listAggregateRowsInWindow`** — `listTransactions`' own `filterConditions`, only the eleven columns a `ReportRow` has, one statement — asserted field for field against `listTransactions` (`packages/contexts/ledger/test/read.test.ts`, including the user-category / rule-category / exclusion tiers). After: dense p95 **100–102 ms**, as generated 34–73 ms (§2). Reports' own loader is unchanged.
- **(b) A render loop in the net worth widget when its source fails.** The card's body (the hero and the chart) are observers of the same query; mounting them over an _errored_ query refetches it (TanStack's retry-on-mount), which put the card back to "loading", unmounted them, failed again and remounted them — forever, pinning the main thread. Found by a flaky `DashboardScreen.test.tsx` (a 50 s stall, CPU profile: `NetWorthWidget` → `useNetWorthSeriesPeriodQuery` → `request` hot). Fixed (a failed read is the card's error state, its body never mounts over it) and pinned by "a failed read is the error state, asked once" (S10).
- **(c) Until the layout is read, the grid mounts nothing.** Drawing the default meanwhile mounted — and fetched for — the very widgets the holder had hidden (`/api/holdings`, `/api/goals`), caught by the hidden-widget test's request log; a placeholder grid now holds the space. A layout that cannot be read falls back to the default.
- Also: the period chips first carried `aria-label="Show the last 3 months"` over visible text "3M" (a 2.5.3 Label in Name failure the sweep checks); the name is now "3M: the last 3 months". The loading regions first carried `aria-label` on a role-less `div` (axe `aria-prohibited-attr`, caught by `networth.spec.ts`); they are `role="status"` now.

**The spending pace, by the dataviz method.** The job is change over time of a running total against a reference, so: lines on one money axis (never two), x = day 1…31. This month solid 2 px in `--chart-expenses`, ending at today with an 8 px dot ringed 2 px in the surface; last month dotted in `--chart-line` at 0.75 opacity (cut at this month's last day so it never runs past the axis); the trailing three-month average a 1 px hairline in `--chart-label`; today's vertical hairline in `--chart-axis`; solid recessive gridlines; compact tabular y ticks; x ticks 1, 8, 15, 22 and the last day (1, 15, last under 260 px) with the ends anchored inward. A legend names the three strokes (solid / dotted / hairline, so identity never rests on hue), a crosshair readout lists every series at the day, **View as table** carries every value (a dash, never $0, for a day that has not happened or a month without that day), and the `aria-label` carries both amounts ("Spending pace for March: $43.00 spent through day 17. By day 17 last month, $90.00. …"). The sentence under the title: "You've spent $X this month. By this day last month you had spent $Y." — then the difference in money with an arrow and a word, in `text-status-success-text` (less) or `text-status-danger-text` (more); never a bare percentage. A last month shorter than today is read at its last day ("By the end of last month (day 28) …"). Nothing animates.

`validate_palette.js --pairs all` on the two coloured series: light `#d2452f,#2563eb` on `#fdfdfc` and dark `#e8604a,#3987e5` on `#191918` — **all checks PASS** (worst CVD ΔE 28.9 / 22.9, normal-vision 36.0 / 31.3). With the average's grey added the validator FAILs the categorical chroma floor (and the dark lightness band) for the grey, and WARNs CVD 7.2 (protan) grey↔vermilion in light: the grey is deliberately not a category but a de-emphasised reference, and it differs from the month by stroke (1 px vs 2 px), the legend and the table — the secondary encoding the 6–8 band requires. Recorded, not hidden. `contrast.test.ts` measures every mark role at 3:1 on every chart surface in both themes (last month **blended at its opacity**) and the tick text at 4.5:1: PASS.

**Spend, exactly.** For each day _d_, `cumulative(d)` is what `GET /reports/spending` reports for `[month start, d]`, negated: the month's rows through `monthActualsOf` (the same `computeActuals` call), each line's transactions summed through day _d_ into the cells Reports would hold, handed to `spendingRowsOf` unchanged. A day with nothing new repeats the day before (`fillEmptyValues`); a month with **no ledger row at all** is returned empty, never as zeros; a month the ledger has rows for with no spend yet is a real 0 ("You've spent $0.00 this month"). The trailing average reads each of the three months at `min(d, its length)`, averages over the months that have rows ("The average is over 1 month: there is no earlier history" otherwise) and rounds half-even once. One currency (the one with most rows); others are named, never summed. `lastSyncedAt` drives an as-of line when the newest sync is over a day old.
