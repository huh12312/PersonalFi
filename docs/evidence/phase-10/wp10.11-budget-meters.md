# WP 10.11: Budget meters, the pace tick, Left to budget and variability (V4 of the visual programme)

**Request (Chris, verbatim):** "Dispatch v1 and once complete continue thru v8" — after "i really like the visuals within monarch money when it comes to spend tracking and other financial health … identify strategic visual improvements to more formally mirror it." V4 is §3's fourth item of `docs/research/10-visual-design-notes.md` (§1.4 "Budget screen": its budget meter on the progress meter with `variant: 'squared'`, `size: 'xs'`, a `tickPercent`, `showRolloverIcon`; the "Left to budget" header card; `budgetVariability` Fixed / Flexible / Non-monthly).

**Result.** The Budget screen opens on a **Left to budget** card: the period's Ready to Assign as the hero figure (sign-coloured; negative keeps WP 2.9's "fix this first" alert in words), "Income not yet given to a category" beneath it, then **Income this period · Budgeted expenses · Planned savings & goals** and a rollover line — every figure a field of `GET /budgets/current`, none computed in the web. Every spending group header carries a **`BudgetMeter`** (one component: sizes `xs | sm | md`, shapes `squared | rounded`) with the share spent, a **pace tick** at today ÷ days in the period (the server's today, in the holder's zone), a rollover glyph when the group carries one, and its state in the V1 status quartets **and in words**: _under pace_ (success), _on pace_ (info, within ±5 points of the tick, inclusive, exact integer arithmetic), _ahead of pace_ (warning), _over budget_ (danger). Every category row carries the `xs` meter; amounts are right-aligned and tabular ("$spent of $budgeted · $left / $over over"). A **By group / By variability** toggle (URL state, `?view=variability`) shows Fixed / Flexible / Non-monthly sections, each with its meter and "Budgeted fixed expenses $X", and an **Unclassified** section with a one-click, keyboard-operable classify control per row; a category an active outflow recurring stream lands in shows **"Suggested: Fixed"** and nothing is written until the holder presses a button. `categories.variability` (migration `0047`, expand-only) is editable through `PATCH /categories/{id}` and round-trips export/import. The dashboard's Budgets widget now uses the same `BudgetMeterRow`. `Badge` and every legacy `--status-good|warning|serious|critical` consumer moved onto the quartets.

**State of the tree.** Uncommitted, on `main` at `794401a` (the dispatch commit; HEAD moved from `514afa4` while this ran). Nothing staged by this WP. Migration `0047_category_variability.sql` is expand-only (DDL in §5). Sixteen PNGs under `docs/evidence/phase-10` (`wp10.3-*` modified, `wp3.11-*` untracked) are not this WP's and were not touched.

**Not done by this WP, by design.**

- **"Income" is the period's income received, not an expected income.** This budget has no expected-income concept; `budget_periods.income_minor` is the actuals pass's income. Labelled "Income this period".
- **The three subordinate figures are context, not Ready to Assign's terms.** Ready to Assign comes from the balance-sheet identity (`@ctx/budgeting` `src/rta.ts`), so income − budgeted expenses − planned savings generally differs from it; the card does not imply an equation.
- **"Group" is today's grouping (by category kind: Spending, Income, Investments, Other, Transfers, Credit card payments), not parent categories.** The brief's "By group (today's view)" names it. Income and card-payment groups have no meter (income is not spent from; a card payment envelope is funded by rule) — their rows are unchanged.
- **Planned savings** = Σ assigned over category lines with class `SAVINGS`, or no class and a `TARGET_BY_DATE` goal (an explicit `NEED`/`WANT` wins). The class is settable through `PATCH /budgets/lines/{id}` but there is no UI for it yet (pre-existing).
- **The suggestion is computed on read**, never persisted: a series has a merchant, not a category, so the category is the modal effective category of that merchant's outflows inside the stream's own span (≤ 400 days).
- **The meter's tooltip** is a CSS hover readout duplicating the meter's `aria-valuetext`; keyboard and screen-reader users get the same words from the meter itself and from "Show the bars as a table".
- No README/RUNBOOK budget section exists to update (checked: neither has one).

### 1.5 The meter, against the dataviz skill

Form: a meter (a single ratio against a limit), not a chart. Fill carries severity; the track is the same quartet's wash, so the state reads across the bar. Status colours are reserved and always paired with an icon and a word. Text wears text roles. Hover readout present; table view present ("Show the bars as a table", with a Pace column). Rendered and looked at: §4.

## 5. Migration DDL

```sql
ALTER TABLE categories
  ADD COLUMN variability TEXT
    CONSTRAINT categories_variability_check
    CHECK (variability IS NULL OR variability IN ('fixed', 'flexible', 'non_monthly'));

COMMENT ON COLUMN categories.variability IS
  'How this category''s spending behaves, as the holder classified it: fixed | flexible | non_monthly. NULL = unclassified. Only the holder sets it (PATCH /categories/{id}); a recurring stream only suggests fixed. WP 10.11.';
```
