# WCAG 2.2 A and AA checklist (WP 9.8)

Every Level A and AA success criterion of WCAG 2.2, how each was verified, the result, and where the evidence is. 4.1.1 Parsing is not
listed: WCAG 2.2 removed it as obsolete, which leaves **55** criteria (31 at A, 24 at AA).

**Tally: 55 criteria. Met 29 (verified, nothing to change). Fixed 15 (a defect found by this WP's checks and corrected in `apps/web/src`; the
check that found it now fails if it comes back). Manual 3 (verified by inspection here; a person's judgement is the real test, listed in
"What is left for Chris" below). N/A 8 (the app has nothing the criterion is about).**

Evidence is in `docs/evidence/phase-9/wp9.8-wcag.md` (§ numbers below refer to it) and in the suites that produce it:
`apps/web/e2e/a11y-sweep.spec.ts` (axe over every route in the route tree, in both themes, with every reachable dialog, tab and chart table
open; 320 px reflow; 200 % zoom and text spacing; reduced motion; labels in names; chart readouts; document titles),
`apps/web/e2e/a11y-keyboard.spec.ts` (keyboard-only journeys and a Tab walk of every screen) and `apps/web/src/styles/contrast.test.ts`
(every colour pair, straight from the tokens). Screenshots of every route in both themes: `wp9.8-<route>-{light,dark}.png`.

How to read "how it is verified": **axe** runs with the WCAG 2.2 A/AA tag set and `target-size` switched on, over `document` (portals included), with
no element excluded; where axe says "incomplete" the harness measures the colour pair itself. **Sweep** is the spec above. **Keyboard** is the
other spec, which cannot touch the pointer (a test reads both files and fails on `mouse`, `click()` and the rest).

## What is left for Chris (not part of "checks pass")

The plan's §6 Q10 default: a screen-reader listening session is **not** claimed here. It is a 30-minute manual item for Chris, with Orca on Firefox
or Chromium, after `just up` on a real stack:

1. Sign in with a passkey; hear the page title change on each route (2.4.2) and the skip link (2.4.1).
2. Transactions: open a row, split it, "Always categorize like this" → Preview → Apply; hear the dialogs open and focus return (1.3.2, 2.4.3, 4.1.2).
3. Budget: edit an envelope; try "five" and hear the alert (3.3.1); hear "Cash overspend" and "Credit overspend" (1.4.1).
4. Brief: Accept one item and Dismiss another with a reason; hear where focus lands afterwards.
5. Plan and Reports: open "View as table" on each chart and read a row (1.1.1); hear the hover readout is not the only way to the numbers.
6. Settings › Crypto: add a wallet on Ethereum and hear the Etherscan note (4.1.3).
7. Privacy mode (`Ctrl+Shift+H`): amounts read "Amount hidden" until focused.
8. Let the soft-lock warning toast appear (14 minutes idle, or shorten it on a dev stack) and hear it (2.2.1, 4.1.3); hear the lock and unlock.
9. A voice-control spot check (2.5.3): say the visible name of five controls ("Search", "Accept", the budget amount, "View as table", "Add goal").

Listed against criteria: **2.4.6** (are the headings and labels descriptive?), **3.3.3** (are the error suggestions helpful?), **4.1.3** (are the status messages
announced at the right moment?). Everything else in the table has an automated or scripted check that fails if it regresses.
