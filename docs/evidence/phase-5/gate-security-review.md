**GO-WITH-CONDITIONS — with three HIGH findings (G5-2, G5-5, G5-7), all of Phase 4's G4-1 class.
Three Phase 5 deliverables are unusable on a shipped install while every test is green; no security
boundary fails open.** WP 9.3, 9.5 and 9.6 carry them, and **the narrator stays off (kill level 2, its
default) until C5-1, C5-2 and C5-4 are closed and walked.**

**What holds, proven by running it.**

- **The kill ladder.** A live narration on the real model was killed at each of the four levels, through the screens, the routes and RUNBOOK's SQL. Each run ended named in 0.41–0.54 s, wrote nothing, and left the Brief, the feed, the health score and the net worth byte-identical. `docker compose stop ollama` failed the run in 0.35 s and the retry in 0.46 s, with the breaker quarantining at three (§3.5).
- **The second exit criterion — zero schema-valid fabricated figures.** It holds in CI, where widening the narrow trace turns the verbatim test red (§2.2 B1), and live, where the gate checked every numeral in every accepted red-team narration (§1.1).
- **The sandbox.** All eleven live checks passed: no `db`, no internet, no secret, non-root, read-only. A planted canary appeared in no log of `worker`, `api` or `ollama`. A re-tagged model was refused before any prompt was sent (§3.6).
- **The scheduled Brief** fired with no user action, published `2026-W41` with `BriefPublished`, and computed the health score in the same job (§3.4).
- **The sabotage log.** 106 sabotages ran; 97 were red on the first valid try. The 9 greens are findings G5-17, G5-18 and G5-19, plus one benign redundancy (§2).
- **The cold install** came up with zero restarts (C-2 closed live), and `check-ci-projects` reads 42/42 and fails when a project is dropped.

**What does not hold.**

- **The narrator cannot be approved through the product** (G5-2): the api binds no renderer, and that was a decision recorded rather than made.
- **A Brief's five-finding narration cannot finish inside 180 s on this host.** It times out, is retried, and quarantines the narrator (G5-7). It narrates the feed rather than the Brief, and nothing could show it anyway (G5-15).
- **The anomaly half of "anomaly and fee hunter" never fires**, because its only scope needs a per-run consent no path supplies (G5-5).
- **The measured 66.1 % was taken on a format the worker does not send** (G5-6). And not one of its 38 refusals is a grounding failure (G5-1).
- **The inspector's "Verified" covers nothing a narrator was sent.** Shown live by tampering the findings and restoring them (G5-16).
- **Smaller gaps.** The consent table's user predicate is untested (G5-18). The Brief's retirement figure is pinned to the engine by a `null = null` test (G5-19). The evidence panel prints `129900` for $1,299.00 (G5-9).

3. **≥ 95 % first-attempt — NOT met** (66.1 % / 69.6 %). Ruled a condition (R-1 → C5-4, WP 9.5 option A), not a mis-set criterion and not a larger model.
4. **≥ 3 useful actions in the Brief's first month — Chris's observation (Q8).** It is now gated on C5-3 and C5-8, so the month measures the Brief and not G5-10's one-purchase "creep" printed in minor units.

**Why GO rather than NO-GO.** Nothing found weakens a boundary: no cross-user read, no model egress, no write after a kill, no fabricated figure. **R-1 — the third exit criterion (≥ 95 % first-attempt) and its refusal classes.** **Not met: 66.1 %
through step 7, 69.6 % through step 5**, re-judged independently and identical (§1.1). The ruling is
**a condition, not NO-GO on the rung and not a mis-set criterion**, for four reasons:

1. **None of the 38 refusals is grounding failure.** 26 are the model failing to copy an opaque key (17 invented keys, 9 prose in the `key` field). 12 are the findings envelope sending `null` for a value the evidence panel shows (8 at step 5, 4 at step 7).

3. **The criterion is sound.** "≥ 95 % pass evidence-ref validation on first attempt" is the right bar for a feature whose every refusal is wasted model time. It stays as written, steps 1–5 reported with steps 1–7 beside it.
4. **The number was measured on a composition no install runs** (G5-6). The worker passes no `format`, and a Brief sends five findings, not one or two (G5-7). Any re-measurement must use the worker's composition.

The remedy is **WP 9.5 option A**: the app writes the key and type (one finding per request, or a numbered reference), and each envelope evidence row carries the value the panel resolves.
