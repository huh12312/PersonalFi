# WP 11.5e: The Brief and the advisor meet ("Talk this through", a plan as tracked progress)

**Request (Chris, verbatim).** 2026-10-25: "Good with the llm proposal but I want to expand askpersonalfi to cover being a financial advisor so come up with an expansion proposal there without requiring a fine tuned model." 2026-10-26: "Good with proposal." WP 11.5e is row 11.5e of `docs/research/12-ask-personalfi-advisor-expansion.md` §6 ("a Brief item can open an advisor session pre-gathered on that finding ('talk this through'); a finished plan appears as tracked progress in the next Briefs"), built to §2.3 step 6 and §3.

**Result.**

1. **"Talk this through"** on a Brief item (and on a pending insight card) opens an advisor session **pre-gathered on that finding**. The question is built by the app from the finding's title and its rule's name, by a template per rule family — never typed, never a model's — and goes through the unchanged pre-check. The session records its origin (migration `0062`: `origin_insight_ref` and `origin_rule_id`, set together or not at all, with `origin_title` and `origin_brief_week`). The clarify step is sent the title and the rule as a **quoted fact** (`about`): a restatement may quote it whole, and a figure lifted out of it is refused. The gather step's compiler still runs, but the rule's **seeded calls always run first** (`mergeSeededCalls`), and the finding's own evidence figures join the context object as result `r1` (`finding.evidence`), each carrying the `source_ref` the validator resolved when the built-in wrote it. The pre-check, the grants, D31's consent, the one-call-per-step claim and every validator apply unchanged.
2. **A plan the holder picked appears as tracked progress in the next Briefs.** The follow-up item now carries, snapshotted on the Brief item (`advisory_brief_items.progress`), the session, the finding that opened it, the day it was picked, the baseline and the current figure and the direction, in **fixed template wording whose every figure is the watcher's own** (held to them by a trace check before it is stored), and a link back to the session. When the watched figure reaches its target the item reads as reached, the session moves to the new terminal status **`completed`** and its watcher stops. When the finding that opened the session is still in the feed, the Brief **dedupes**: the progress item wins (it stands in at the higher of the two ranks) and the finding is marked "being worked on" instead of being shown twice.
3. **Web** (by a Sonnet-tier sub-agent; §1.6): the link, the origin header, the progress card, the completed state, the keyboard journey, the screenshots.
4. **Evaluation**: six pre-gathered sessions in the advisor golden set, one per rule family; the seeded calls asserted present and leading.
5. **Red team**: two probes (a finding title carrying an injected instruction; evidence figures carrying a merchant name).
6. **Docs**: RUNBOOK "The Brief and the advisor meet (v0.9.25)" under "The advisor", release notes `## v0.9.25` naming the migration, 12 §6 row 11.5e, the migrate test's narrative, the soak-canary classification.

**Nothing new leaves the machine, so there is no new control check.** The pre-gathered session sends the same payload classes 11.5d's protocol already sends, under the same approvals: the question (now built from the finding's title), the finding's title and rule words (`about`, in clarify), and figures the holder has granted the language model (the finding's own evidence figures are admitted only when the finding's source scopes are all within the narrator grant — checked at `POST` and again at gather). No adapter, prompt or template hash changed, so no existing approval is invalidated. The sixteen self-test checks are unchanged.

**No live model was called.** Every model in this package's tests is the Ollama fake or a recorded reply.

**State of the tree.** Uncommitted; nothing staged by this WP. HEAD was `ceac8bd` when I started and is `5d5ac10` now (the orchestrator's own commits). ### 1.1 The pre-gather table (rule family → question template → seeded calls)

The question template, verbatim; `{title}` is the finding's title (a goal name's digits redacted, as every free-text reader sees it), `{rule}` the rule's display name.

| Family        | Rules                                                                                 | Question template                                                                                                        | Seeded calls (parsed through the function's own schema)                                                                                                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| cash          | C1, C2, C3, C4, C7                                                                    | `My Brief flagged “{title}” ({rule}). What could I do about my cash cushion, and what would each choice cost?`           | `cash.emergency_fund {target_months: 3}`                                                                                                                 |
| debt          | D1–D6                                                                                 | `My Brief flagged “{title}” ({rule}). What are my options for these debts, and what would each choice cost?`             | `debt.payoff_plan {order: avalanche, extra_monthly: $0.00}` (both orders come back side by side)                                                         |
| investing     | I1 → drift, I2 → costs, I3 → allocation, I4/I5 → concentration, I6 → allocation       | `My Brief flagged “{title}” ({rule}). What are my options for my investments here, and what would each choice cost?`     | `investing.drift` / `.costs` / `.allocation` / `.concentration` `{}`                                                                                     |
| subscriptions | U2, U7 (U3 is in the table but reads `transactions.raw`, so it never offers the link) | `My Brief flagged “{title}” ({rule}). What are my options for these recurring charges, and what would each choice cost?` | `recurring.waste {}`                                                                                                                                     |
| retirement    | R1 → plan.project, R2 → plan.levers, R3 → rmd_window, R4 → claiming, R7 → guardrail   | `My Brief flagged “{title}” ({rule}). What are my options for my retirement plan, and what would each choice cost?`      | `plan.project {scenario: baseline}`, `plan.levers {}`, `retirement.rmd_window {}`, `retirement.claiming {ages: [62, 67, 70]}`, `retirement.guardrail {}` |
| goals         | R6                                                                                    | `My Brief flagged “{title}” ({rule}). What are my options for reaching this goal, and what would each choice cost?`      | `goal.required_savings {goal_ref}` — the ref the finding's key carries (`retirement.goal_pace.GOAL_…`); none is guessed when it is absent                |

No other rule (the spending rules, crypto, anomalies, the rule suggester) offers the link: `catalogue/1` has no function that answers them. The merge: every seeded call first, then the compiler's calls that are not the same call again (name + canonical arguments), up to the step's cap of four; a compiled call past the cap is dropped and counted (`compiled_dropped`).

**Which evidence rows join the context object** (`findingEvidenceFigures`, each other row dropped with its reason): a `source_ref` that is a snapshot path (`source_ref_not_a_snapshot_path`), not an identity field — `.name`, `.merchant_ref`, `.liability_ref`, `.goal_ref`, `.payee`, `.description`, `.memo`, `.institution` (`identity_field`) —, **that resolved** (`insight_evidence.resolved_display` recorded: `did_not_resolve`), whose shown text is a figure as the app writes one (money, a percent, a number, months/years/days, an age, a date: `not_a_figure`), under a label with no pseudonym, no markup, no run of three digits and no other row's non-figure value (`label_refused`). At most twelve. Then, at gather, all of the finding's source scopes must be in the holder's narrator grant (`scope_not_granted`), and `findingFiguresResolve` re-checks every figure's single `source_ref` against the finding's resolved rows (`unresolved`) before `r1` is added. The session records the outcome (`pregathered.finding`: `joined` or why not).

### 1.2 The progress wording (templates, verbatim)

`PROGRESS_TEMPLATES` (`{on}` is ` on <the day picked>` or nothing; `{moved}` is `down since then` / `up since then` / `no change since then`):

| State    | Template                                                                                                                 |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| tracking | `{label} was {baseline} when you picked this plan{on}. It is {latest} now: {moved}.`                                     |
| reached  | `{label} was {baseline} when you picked this plan{on}. It is {latest} now, which reaches the plan’s target of {target}.` |
| not_read | `{label} was {baseline} when you picked this plan{on}. It could not be read this time.`                                  |

Every numeral and number word of the filled sentence must be one of the watcher's own figure texts (baseline, latest, target) or the pick date (`progressWordingTraces`, the narrator's own `untraceableInFinding` / `untraceableWordFiguresInFinding`); a sentence that fails is never stored — `You are following a plan you picked in an advisor session.` (no figure) stands instead. The targets (`WATCHER_TARGETS`): a goal's extra a month needed → $0.00; the debts in the payoff plan → $0.00; the year's contribution room → $0.00; a generic figure → none (never completes). "Reached" is a decimal comparison of the watcher's own values (`latest ≤ target`), never the text. Example (the api-db journey): `Needed per month was $1,089.89 when you picked this plan on 2026-10-29. It is $1,089.89 now: no change since then.`, then after the windfall `…It is $0.00 now, which reaches the plan’s target of $0.00.`

### 1.3 The dedupe (the Brief's candidate selection)

`assembleBrief`: progress candidates in `key` order each claim the finding their origin names, if that finding is itself an eligible candidate this week (first claim wins). A claimed finding is withdrawn from the ranked list and the attention strip. Its progress item is scored at the higher of its own score and the finding's — with that entry's factors, so the stored score still equals its factors — and, when neither has a dollar figure and the finding was on the attention strip, takes its place there. The progress item's `workedOn` names the finding; `progressRowOf` stores it as `progress.worked_on`, and the Brief card shows "Being worked on".

## 8. For the orchestrator and Chris

- **Four origin columns, not two.** The pair the dispatch names (`origin_insight_ref`, `origin_rule_id`) is held together by its own CHECK; the title as the holder saw it (`origin_title`, riding with the pair) and the Brief week (`origin_brief_week`, only with a finding) were added so the advisor's header and the progress item never re-read a live insight whose title the nightly upsert may since have changed.
- **The goal watcher's target is "no extra a month needed"**: `goal.required_savings` solves the extra beyond the plan's own flows, so a goal contribution plan alone does not move it (the journey found this — the first completion attempt with a $6,000 a month contribution plan left it at $1,089.89); a plan that funds the goal (here a windfall life event) does.
- **The progress item stands in at the higher of its own rank and the finding's** (with that entry's factors) so a finding being worked on never simply vanishes from the Brief; with neither carrying a dollar figure it takes the finding's attention-strip slot.

- **`progress` is exported with the Brief items**; the session it points at is not (11.5d's exemption), so after a restore "Open the plan" finds no session.
- **The api-db suite republishes "the next Brief" by withdrawing this week's row** (a test-only `DELETE`), because the weekly job is idempotent per ISO week and the clock is the system's.
